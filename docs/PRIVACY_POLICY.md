# Tash Rush Privacy Policy — publication draft

Effective date: [PUBLISH_DATE]

Tash Rush stores game progress, settings, achievements, daily challenge data,
and personal score history locally on the player's device.

When the production Firebase configuration is enabled, Firebase Analytics may
receive gameplay events such as a game start, finish, score milestone, daily
challenge completion, and achievement unlock. Firebase Crashlytics may receive
crash diagnostics, device information, and technical logs needed to investigate
failures. This data is used to maintain and improve the game.

When production advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process device,
advertising, consent, and ad-interaction information. Ads are initialized only
after the Google User Messaging Platform consent flow says ads may be requested.
Rewarded ads are optional. Interstitial ads appear only at natural pauses.

Tash Rush does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names,
email addresses, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment-card data.
Local game data can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the
app. Consent choices can be reviewed from Settings when required in the user's
region.

Google and Firebase process data under their own terms and privacy policies.
Retention and deletion of data handled by those services follow the production
project settings and the providers' policies.
